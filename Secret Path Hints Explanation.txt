Secret Path 1: Answer "No" 5 times to the sign at the end of the gray path that becomes darker.
Secret Path 2: When you arrive at the graveyard after reaching the sign for the second time and answering yes, rearrange the Unown in your party to spell DIEDHE and then walk around until you start spinning. You'll end up in a grave spiral if you've done it correctly.
Secret Path 3: After spinning down in the graveyard for the second time (without needing to rearrange the Unown), interact with the very tall red pillar at the start of the long red hallway.
Secret Path 4: After you've finished all 3 previous paths and defeated Red at the end of the long red hallway, you'll end up at your house. After exiting your house and walking through the darkness, a bloody floor will be visible on the side. Keep walking until you can't, then turn around.
